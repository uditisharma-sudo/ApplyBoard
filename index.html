Executive Summary
8 critical insights for CEO & leadership decision-making — West Africa UK Programme Portfolio
1

🚨 Catastrophic End-to-End Conversion: Only 1.43% of Applications Result in a Visa

From 45,397 applications, only 647 students obtained a visa — a 1.43% end-to-end conversion. This reveals a structurally broken pipeline: massive front-end volume is masking severe leakage at submission, offer, and visa stages. The business is built on a false funnel.

2

🔥 331 Programmes Generating Zero Offers — 5,647 Applications Wasted

331 programmes with 5+ applications each produced literally zero LOAs/offers. These consumed counsellor time, BDM effort, and marketing spend with zero revenue return. 12.4% of all applications flowed into dead-end programmes. This is the single biggest efficiency drain in the portfolio.

3

⚠️ University of Hertfordshire: #1 Volume, Bottom-Tier ROI

Hertfordshire leads with 5,386 applications but achieves only a 0.93% visa conversion rate — far below peers. Despite being the highest-volume partner, it contributes just 50 visas. Teesside (2,389 apps) produces 68 visas at 2.85% E2E conversion. Resources are misallocated to volume over value.

4

⚠️ Research Degrees Are a Resource Trap — 9.3% Offer Rate, 0.45% E2E Conversion

Research-by-degree programmes absorb 3,737 applications (8.2% of total) but return only 17 visas — an abysmal 0.45% E2E rate. Universities appear to accept "MRes/MPhil by Research" applications opportunistically; actual conversions are near-zero. These programmes should be delisted from promotional materials immediately.

5

✅ Public Health & Engineering Are Hidden High-Performers

Public Health (2.15% E2E) and Engineering (2.31% E2E) significantly outperform the portfolio average of 1.43%. With comparatively lower application volumes, these categories offer the best return per counsellor hour. Doubling focus on these categories could materially increase revenue without increasing headcount.

6

✅ University of Sunderland Is the Star Partner — 3.52% E2E Conversion

Sunderland delivers 66 visas from 1,877 applications at 3.52% E2E — the best conversion among high-volume universities. The Nursing and International Business Management programmes are standout performers. This university deserves priority BDM attention and marketing investment.

7

📊 Extreme Revenue Concentration: 226 Programmes (4.1%) Drive 80% of Visa Outcomes

This Pareto imbalance exposes severe portfolio risk. If even 10–15 of these top programmes close or reduce intake, the business loses a disproportionate share of revenue. Simultaneously, thousands of programmes sit in the portfolio consuming effort but generating zero results.

8

📈 Submission Gap: 41% of Applications Never Get Submitted — First Major Leak

26,809 submissions out of 45,397 applications means 41% of applicants never reach a university submission. This first-stage drop is either a counsellor capacity problem, applicant unreadiness, or a programme-market mismatch. Fixing this single stage could unlock significant revenue without adding new partners.
